Guardrails and transparency
Responsible AI

FitPilot combines explicit planning rules with grounded explanations to keep the routine transparent and explainable. It is designed for general wellness guidance, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

  • Schedules are validated against user time constraints and work blocks.
  • Selected preferences and activities influence the plan without overriding hard constraints.
  • AI explanations are grounded in the actual activity library and the current schedule.
  • Users should choose activities appropriate to their conditions and stop if they feel pain or discomfort.
